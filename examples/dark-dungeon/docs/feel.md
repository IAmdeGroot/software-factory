# Dark Dungeon — Feel

Human wish **WISH-001**: inspired by Hollow Knight, not a clone.

## Keep

- Godot 4 **top-down** 2D (the scaffold already runs this way)
- One bead, one playable increment
- Nail-like melee: short, directional, committed

## Borrow from Hollow Knight

- A **nail**: attack in the facing direction, not a 360° aura
- Lonely insect-ruin mood (palette, silhouettes) as content grows
- Later: rooms that ask you to come back with a new move (not this increment)

## Do not

- Switch to a side-scroller or copy Hallownest
- Add pogo, soul spells, charms, or a full map in the next beads
- Spend a session on “atmosphere only” with no new verb

Next playable verbs: a doorway / second room, not a Hallownest map.
