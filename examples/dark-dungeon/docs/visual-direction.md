# Dark Dungeon — Visual Direction

## The look: drowned reliquary

Dark Dungeon is an original top-down dark-fantasy world built from inked
silhouettes, old mineral color, and small living lights. It should feel
hand-authored and strange rather than medieval-generic.

Hollow Knight is a reference for silhouette clarity and melancholy. Elden
Ring is a reference for age, scale, and tarnished grandeur. We do not copy
their characters, iconography, architecture, palettes, or UI.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Void ink | `#090B13` | outlines, deep gaps |
| Moon stone | `#151827` | floor and architecture |
| Oxidized teal | `#315653` | age, magic, quiet life |
| Tarnished gold | `#A87B42` | doors, goals, interaction |
| Ember wine | `#6E2937` | danger and damaged foes |
| Ghost cyan | `#91DBC7` | player eyes, shards, sacred light |
| Bone | `#D8D0B2` | player face and readable highlights |

## Shape language

- The player is narrow and pale-faced, with an asymmetric ink-dark cloak.
- Common foes are low, many-legged silhouettes; each has a distinct mass.
- Sacred objects use vertical symmetry, teal cores, and tarnished-gold lines.
- The warden is broad and crown-like, but not a human knight.
- Architecture is heavy and rectangular; cracks, patina, and inlays break it.

## Readability hierarchy

1. Ghost cyan and bone: player, pickups, critical magic.
2. Warm gold and amber: interaction and navigation.
3. Saturated wine/purple: threats.
4. Blue-black stone: environment.

Combat actors retain dark outlines and brighter eyes against every floor.
Texture stays low contrast beneath the playable path.

## Motion and finish

Lighting, shadow, haze, and parallax are later passes. They should deepen this
palette, not replace it. Effects must never hide hitboxes, exits, or threats.
